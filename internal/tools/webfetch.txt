## webfetch

Fetches a web page and returns its content as plain text.
Private, loopback, and RFC1918 addresses are blocked (SSRF protection).

Parameters:
- `url` (required, string): URL to fetch (http/https only)
- `max_bytes` (optional, integer): maximum bytes to read (default: 65536, max: 1048576)

Output is truncated at 64 KiB.
